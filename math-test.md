# Maxwell's equations — differential form (SI, general material form)

1. $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho$ (Gauss's law for electric field)

2. $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$ (Gauss's law for magnetism — no magnetic monopoles)

3. $\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$ (Faraday's law of induction)

4. $\nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}$ (Ampère–Maxwell law)
